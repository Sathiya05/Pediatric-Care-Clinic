Our Child-Friendly Approach

Small humans, big feelings — handled gently.

Every LittleSteps doctor practices the same exam playbook designed to lower fear and build trust:

  • Tour the room with your child before anything happens
  • “Warm-up” tools — exam with the plush bunny first
  • Choices wherever possible: which arm, which sticker, which song
  • Honesty about pokes and prods — never surprises

What to Expect During a Consultation

  1. 1Hello & comfort. Your child settles in with a book or toy; we chat with you about sleep, feeding and mood.
  2. 2Gentle exam. Heart, lungs, ears, reflexes — explained step by step in kid-friendly words.
  3. 3Growth review. Height, weight and milestones plotted and discussed together.
  4. 4Plan & take-home. Clear written summary, vaccines due, follow-ups — and stickers well earned.
