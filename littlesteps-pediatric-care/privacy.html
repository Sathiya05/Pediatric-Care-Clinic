Privacy Policy (Demo)

This website is a frontend demonstration. It is designed to show a complete pediatric clinic experience — it is not a live clinical service.

What this demo stores, and where

Appointment requests, demo child profiles, demo vaccination records, demo growth measurements, theme and layout preferences, and demo messages are stored only in your browser’s localStorage on your device. Nothing is transmitted to any LittleSteps server, because no server exists in this demo.

No real medical data

All sample records are fictional and clearly labelled as demo content. Do not enter real medical information into this website. localStorage is not encrypted and must never be treated as secure medical record storage.

Cookies & third parties

This demo sets no tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; maps are embedded from OpenStreetMap. If you self-host or modify the project, update this section accordingly.

Your choices

You can erase all demo data at any time using “Reset Demo Data” in either dashboard, or by clearing your browser’s site data. Theme and direction preferences are also stored locally and can be reset the same way.

Production requirements

A real deployment must add: encrypted storage, role-based access control on a server, audit logging, data-processing agreements, consent flows, retention policies, and compliance with applicable health-data laws (e.g., HIPAA-type frameworks).
