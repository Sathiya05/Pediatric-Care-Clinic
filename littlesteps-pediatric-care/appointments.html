    Before Your Visit — Prep Guide

    • Bring immunization records and a list of current medicines
    • Pack a small comfort kit: snack, toy, wipes, extra clothes
    • Note questions beforehand — feeding, sleep, behavior, growth
    • Arrive 10 minutes early for calm check-in

    What Happens After Booking

    1. Our team reviews your request (demo: it appears in the admin dashboard).
    2. You receive a confirmation by phone or email within one working day.
    3. A reminder goes out the day before your visit.
    4. After the visit, follow-ups appear in your parent dashboard.

    Urgent or Emergency?

    If your child has difficulty breathing, severe allergic reaction, seizures, or unresponsiveness — call your local emergency number (911 in the US) or go to the nearest ER immediately. Do not book a routine appointment for emergencies.
