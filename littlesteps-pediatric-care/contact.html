Frequently Asked Questions

Before you call…

Appointments are strongly recommended so we can give your child unhurried time. Same-day sick slots are usually kept during weekday mornings.

Immunization records, a list of current medicines or supplements, your insurance card (if applicable), and your child — ideally fed and rested.

We reserve a limited number of same-day slots for fever, infections and urgent concerns. Call early — once filled, we’ll offer the next earliest slot.

For emergencies — breathing difficulty, severe allergic reactions, seizures, unresponsiveness — call your local emergency number (911 in the US) immediately. Do not wait for a clinic callback.
