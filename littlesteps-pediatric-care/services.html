Well-Child Visits

Routine, age-based checkups are the single most powerful habit for lifelong health. We track development, answer parent questions, and catch concerns early.

  • Full physical exam, immunization review and developmental screening
  • Vision, hearing and oral-health screening appropriate to age
  • Anticipatory guidance: sleep, nutrition, screen time and safety

Vaccination Services

Age-appropriate immunization support, delivered with patience and clear explanations. We review your child’s documented schedule and answer every question honestly.

  • Friendly, anxiety-free vaccine visits with comfort techniques
  • Personal immunization record you can keep and share
  • We always confirm timing with your child’s pediatrician and current official national guidance

Growth Monitoring

  • Personal growth charts reviewed together with you
  • Early flagging of patterns worth a closer look
  • Records you can track in the demo parent dashboard

Developmental Screening

Gentle, play-based snapshots of speech, movement, learning and social skills at the ages when each skill typically blooms.

  • Standardized screening tools for speech, motor and social milestones
  • Clear written results and practical next steps when needed
  • Coordinated referral guidance to specialists if screening suggests it
